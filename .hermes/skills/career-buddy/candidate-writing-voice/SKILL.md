# the user Writing Voice

## When to Use
Apply when Writer drafts or edits documents in the user’s voice: CVs, cover letters, selection criteria, recruiter and application messages, interview answers, and related workplace or casual messages. This is a voice layer, not a document-generation workflow. Other instructions define document structure, selection requirements, layout and submission rules. Never force conversational prose into CV bullets or override required terminology.

## Procedure
1. Read [the approved profile](references/voice-profile.md) and relevant context rules in [modes](references/modes.md). For calibration, read the matching [approved examples](references/examples.md). Consult [decision history](references/decisions.md) when a preference is ambiguous.
2. Use the supplied document brief, recipient context and verified evidence. The fact bank (`private/facts/`) is the authority for career facts; this skill has no verified copy of it. Do not treat examples as evidence of achievements, qualifications, work rights or availability. If a key fact or recipient need is missing, ask one concise question before writing the dependent content. Do not block on optional details.
3. Preserve meaning, attribution, certainty and factual scope. Rephrase freely for clarity and a concrete contribution, but add specifics only when supported. Preserve required technical terms and employer wording when accurate; do not copy claims from the advert into the user’s history.
4. Write natural Australian English at C1: familiar precise words, correct grammar, practical explanations and a generally short sentence rhythm with natural variation. Keep the user’s warmth and directness. Help the reader understand his contribution without turning every opening into a sales pitch.
5. Normally present two complete, meaningfully different voice variants grounded in the same facts. Differences may be warmth, directness or rhythm, not invented achievements or changed commitments. For long documents, provide two versions if requested; agree a smaller representative passage if necessary before applying a voice throughout. If the user explicitly requests one final version, follow that request. Do not add English lessons unless asked.
6. On an explicit choice or edit, automatically record the preference in [the decision record](references/decisions.md), following its protocol, using an authorised persistent workspace. No repeat confirmation is needed for recording the choice. An unselected draft, silence or an unrelated follow-up is not approval. Contextual choices do not establish universal bans. Never claim persistence if storage is unavailable; expose the unsaved update to Writer for later persistence.
7. Review using Verification below. Output the requested document, not the internal analysis. No automatic sending, application submission or infrastructure changes.

## Pitfalls
- Robotic precision means excessively calculated prose, not factual accuracy. Do not inject mistakes, random informality, false hesitation or deliberate inconsistency to sound human.
- C1 is a communication target, not a blacklist of advanced vocabulary or a measured assessment of the user’s level.
- Avoid empty corporate adjectives, inflated self-praise, formulaic enthusiasm and repetitive polished paragraphs. Evaluate phrases in context; do not ban all idioms or all wording used by AI.
- “It is about helping the other person” does not erase the user’s contribution or prevent “I’m looking for a new role”.
- Do not invent team needs, figures, credentials, availability, legal work rights or a guarantee. Never change “helped” to “led”, “could” to “will”, or shared work to individual ownership without evidence.
- Do not modify the fact bank (`private/facts/`) or Hermes configuration as part of applying this skill.

## Verification
Check factual fidelity and certainty first; then reader relevance, C1 clarity, Australian spelling, grammar, context-appropriate professionalism, personal warmth and non-mechanical rhythm. Confirm formal requirements survived the voice edit. Use [quality tests](references/quality-tests.md) for reproducible evaluations; resemblance requires the user’s judgement, not an invented accuracy score.

For Writer integration and version limitations, read [the handoff](references/writer-handoff.md). Current learning data is local text, not autonomous model training.
