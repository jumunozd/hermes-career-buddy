# P-2 Early hiring contacts: Job Hunter + Research Agent (status: approved 2026-11-15, AP-ea17780d4846d8c90c64ab11)

Source: user request 2026-11-15 and the user's draft skill `early-hiring-and-direct-outreach` v1.0
(controlled copy: `knowledge/proposed-skills/early-hiring-and-direct-outreach.v1.0.md`, sha256 5a79762d7323b378).

## Problem
Speed matters: applying within 24 h and reaching the right person the same day improves the user's chances
(D-USR-01). Today the daily digest only shows contacts printed in the ad. Nobody finds the hiring
manager when the ad names no one, and no short outreach note is prepared.

## Evidence
- RT-08/RT-10: Shaw and TasPorts ads named a contact or channel, and `bin/hcb contacts` extracted it for free.
- The draft skill's own provenance says the "24 h / <100 likes / 5-line email" method is an anecdote
  without a controlled comparison. This proposal adopts it as a hypothesis and measures it (section 9).

## Change

### 1. Who does what
| Step | Owner | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts printed in the ad (name, phone, email, "no approaches" clause) | **Job Hunter**, deterministic `bin/hcb contacts` | ~0 tokens |
| Up to 2 people when the ad names fewer than 2 | **Research Agent**, new action `contact_research` | LLM + web |
| 3–5-line outreach draft | **Writer** with candidate-writing-voice, only on `Prepara contacto [ID]` | LLM |
| Integrity check of the draft (G1 claims only) | **Recruiter**, short mode | LLM |
| Sending: email, LinkedIn, call | **The user only** | — |

Why Research and not the Job Hunter: the Job Hunter must stay fast and cheap (two runs a day). People
research uses different sources and stricter privacy rules. A separate action can be triggered only for
the offers that deserve it, and the Job Hunter specification already forbids contact work (JH §2.4).

### 2. Trigger (token cap)
Research `contact_research` runs only when every one of these holds:
1. New in this run and published ≤ 24 h ago (or date unknown and first seen today).
2. Category `principal`, or `exploratoria` with Entry Signal = Strategic Entry.
3. A–H is not DO_NOT_RECOMMEND (unless the user overrode it).
4. No "no approaches / canvassing will disqualify / no agencies" clause in the ad (deterministic check).
5. The ad names fewer than 2 suitable contacts.
6. At most 3 offers per run. The rest go to the digest without research.

### 3. Who to contact (maximum 2, in this order)
1. **The person the ad names** (contact officer, "enquiries to", agency consultant). The employer invited
   that contact, so it always comes first, and calling beats email when a phone number is given.
2. **The hiring manager**: the role the position reports to ("reports to the Manager Project Controls"),
   identified on a public source (employer site, annual report, media release, public profile found by search).
3. **Fallback only if 1 or 2 is missing:** the internal talent-acquisition partner for that business unit,
   then the official careers inbox.

Special cases:
- **Agency ad:** the agency consultant only. Never go around the agency to its client.
- **Government or GBE (TasPorts, councils, State Service):** the named contact officer only. Never panel
  members, and never canvassing.
- **The formal channel always applies:** the outreach complements the portal application and never replaces it.

### 4. Contact data rules (stricter than the draft)
- Only business contact details published by the employer or by the person in a professional context.
  **No personal email addresses, no guessed address patterns, no enrichment services.**
  `email_status: verified_public_business | not_verified`.
- LinkedIn: public search results and profile URLs only. No logged-in scraping and no automation (ToS).
  the user opens the profile and sends any note himself.
- Stored per contact: name, current title, employer, why relevant, source URL, observed date and
  confidence (`confirmed | inferred`). Nothing else about the person.
- A LinkedIn profile does not prove someone is the hiring manager: mark it `inferred`.

### 5. Outputs
- The daily digest adds one line per contact, e.g.
  `👤 Jane Doe, Manager Project Controls (fuente: tasports.com.au, inferred) · LinkedIn`.
- The research brief uses the draft skill's §8 fields, trimmed to what the digest and Writer need, and is
  saved under `state/research/<opp>/contacts.json`.
- Baserow: `[contact]` in the Notes tag block today; contact fields are added to MIG-01 (needs approval).
  No new table.

### 6. Integration with the 05:00 / 21:00 schedule
The 05:00 run finishes discovery and then research, so by about 06:00 the user has the digest with
contacts and can apply and call or email in business hours the same day. The 21:00 run catches
same-day ads for the next morning.

### 7. Specification amendments this needs (the user's approval = this proposal)
- research-agent.md §0 rule 9 ("no research on private persons") gains a narrow exception: the
  public professional role of at most 2 people tied to a specific vacancy, limited to the fields in section 4.
  Panel members stay excluded.
- research-agent.md pitfall "deep research on every opportunity" is replaced by the trigger in section 2.
- The draft skill says the Recruiter composes messages. Here the **Writer** composes and the Recruiter
  checks, as in the rest of the system.
- The draft skill's role families yield to `config/job-targets.yaml` (the draft itself says to escalate conflicts).
- Unchanged: the system never sends or contacts (D-PM-03); the 10-day follow-up stays.

## Risk
- **Annoying a hiring manager, or breaching a canvassing rule:** the clause check, the government/agency
  rules and the 2-contact cap limit this, and the user decides every send.
- **Wrong person:** `inferred` label plus a source; the user verifies before writing.
- **Token cost:** research cost per offer is unknown until RT-12 measures it, and the cap of 3 per run bounds it.
- **web_discovery is OFF today.** Research needs Hermes web tools enabled for this action only.

## Success criteria
RT-12 covers the 8 acceptance tests of the draft skill plus: a no-approaches clause, an agency ad, a
government ad, and the TasPorts ad. Corrected on implementation (2026-11-15): TasPorts is state-owned,
so the government rule of section 3 applies. Expected: only the ad's Recruitment inbox
(recruitment@tasports.com.au, confirmed), no people research, no hiring manager. The earlier wording
("reports to" manager second) contradicted section 3. Doctor green; no outbound message possible from any tool.

## Measurement (after go-live, from Baserow)
Contacts proposed, contacts the user used, replies, conversations and interviews, against applications
without outreach. Review after about 20 outreach cases. Keep or drop the method based on that.

## Rollback
`features.contact_research: false` turns the action off; the digest falls back to ad-printed contacts.
Skill and code are versioned; the spec amendments revert with the proposal.
